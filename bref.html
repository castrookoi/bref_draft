About Us

Who We Are

BREF RESOURCES LIMITED is a fully indigenous Nigerian company incorporated in 2008. We specialise in engineering, maritime, logistics, environmental, and oilfield support services.

Our focus is on practical, coordinated solutions that help clients maintain operational continuity in offshore, swamp, coastal, and industrial environments. We partner closely with oilfield operators, EPC contractors, and industrial players to deliver dependable marine assets, specialized logistics, technical procurement, and field support tailored to each project.

As a 100% indigenous enterprise, we are positioned to support local content development and participate meaningfully in Nigeria’s energy, marine, and industrial growth.

Governance & Responsibility

Corporate Policies

Our policies reflect our institutional commitment to safety, continuous quality, environmental stewardship, and ethical business conduct.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Policy

We target zero harm to people, assets, and the natural ecosystem. All personnel are empowered with "Stop Work Authority", report hazards promptly, and adhere to Nigerian and international maritime safety regulations. Continuous refinement of our HSE management system is a daily priority.

Quality Assurance & Quality Control (QA/QC)

We deliver services that consistently meet or surpass client expectations. Quality standards are integrated into planning, OEM sourcing, logistics deployment, and handover. Performance indicators are tracked systematically to ensure excellence.

Environmental Sustainability Policy

We minimize the ecological footprint of our operations through fuel efficiency, certified waste segregation, spill response preparedness, and strict compliance with Nigerian environmental standards across offshore and coastal zones.

Local Content & Indigenous Participation

As a 100% indigenous enterprise, we prioritize Nigerian engineering talent, local subcontracting, and host community youth empowerment in strict alignment with the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development (NOGICD) Act.

Business Ethics & Anti-Corruption Policy

We conduct all corporate affairs with unwavering transparency, fairness, and zero tolerance for bribery, conflict of interest, or unfair trade practices. We enforce identical anti-corruption protocols across our suppliers, subcontractors, and commercial partners.
